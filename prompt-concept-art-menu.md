# Prompts de concept art: fundo do menu inicial

Três prompts para gerar no ChatGPT (gerador de imagens) a arte de fundo do menu inicial de **Grim Cubes**. A imagem não leva botões nem textos de menu, só o título do jogo. A UI do menu é desenhada por cima pelo jogo.

## Como usar

- Cole um prompt por vez, em inglês (o gerador segue melhor), e peça variações do que gostar.
- Formato: paisagem 16:9. Peça 1920×1080 ou maior; o jogo usa em 1920×1080 e 2560×1440 e corta para 896×414 e 800×360 no celular, então o foco (herói e título) deve ficar no centro.
- Deixe uma faixa mais escura e calma num dos lados (indicada em cada prompt) para os botões do menu ficarem legíveis por cima.
- A referência às capas de DOOM é de **estilo** (composição heroica, herói contra a horda, pintura épica, inferno em brasa). Não use logos, nomes, armas ou personagens dessas marcas. Se o gerador puser texto além do título, peça para remover.
- O título deve sair escrito exatamente **GRIM CUBES**. Se sair errado, peça para corrigir só o título ou gere sem título ("no text") e aplique o logo depois.

## Dados do jogo usados nos prompts

- Herói: **o Carrasco**, executor condenado, armadura de placas enferrujadas, capuz de ferro, olhos em brasa sob o capuz, espada de carrasco larga.
- Mundo: 3D voxel (tudo feito de cubos, estilo Minecraft/Hytale mais detalhado), grimdark.
- Inimigos: mortos-vivos de osso (Carcaças), crânios gritando (Lamentos), sanguessugas de carne, cães de ferro com espinhos (Caçadores), um ferreiro gigante com fornalha no peito (Forjador).
- Paleta (Art Bible §3): osso `#D9C9A3`, ferro `#5A5F66`, ferrugem `#8A4A2B`, sangue quase vinho `#5E0E14`, brasa `#E07A2A` e `#F5B04A`, noite `#14121C`, sombra arroxeada `#2B2340`, névoa `#3F3A52`. Sem branco nem preto puros; sombras com matiz azul ou roxo.

## Prompt 1: O Carrasco sobre a pilha de mortos

Composição clássica de capa: o herói em pé, de frente, dominando a cena, a horda vindo de baixo.

```text
Epic video game cover art, digital painting, landscape 16:9. Inspired by the composition and mood of classic 1990s and modern heavy-metal shooter box art (heroic low angle, lone warrior against an endless demonic horde, hellish sky), but entirely original.

The world is made of voxels: every character and object is built from chunky cubes, like a highly detailed, dark Minecraft/Hytale style, rendered as a dramatic painted illustration with volumetric light.

Center: "the Executioner", a massive armored warrior seen from a low angle, standing on top of a mound of broken voxel skeletons and bone cubes. Rusted iron plate armor, a heavy iron hood that hides the face except two glowing ember-orange eyes, a huge broad executioner's sword resting on his shoulder, tattered dark cloth hanging from the belt, bone trophies and chains.

Below and around him: a horde of voxel undead climbing toward him: skeletal husks, screaming skull-headed ghouls with open jaws, pale segmented leech-worms, spiked iron hounds with ember eyes. Blood is dark wine red, never bright red.

Background: a sunken graveyard under a dead sky, broken tombstones and crosses, a distant giant furnace-chested blacksmith silhouette glowing orange, embers and ash falling, purple-violet fog.

Palette: bone beige #D9C9A3, iron grey #5A5F66, rust #8A4A2B, wine blood #5E0E14, ember orange #E07A2A and #F5B04A, night #14121C, violet shadows #2B2340. No pure white, no pure black; shadows tinted purple-blue.

Title: the words "GRIM CUBES" at the top center, in a heavy, angular, metallic logo with chiseled bevels, rust and ember glow in the cracks, built from cube shapes. No other text, no menus, no buttons, no UI.

Keep the left third of the image darker and calmer (fog and shadow) so menu buttons can sit on top. Grimdark, brutal, heroic, high detail, cinematic lighting from below by fire.
```

## Prompt 2: A forja infernal

Mais próximo da era "eterna" das capas: mundo em chamas, cores de brasa dominando, herói de três quartos avançando para o leitor.

```text
Original epic game key art, landscape 16:9, painterly and cinematic. Mood inspired by modern hell-themed heavy-metal shooter covers: an infernal world on fire, a single armored slayer charging forward, sparks and molten metal everywhere. Do not copy any existing logo, character or weapon.

Everything is voxel-based: chunky cube-built characters, rocks, chains and machines, like a richly detailed dark Minecraft/Hytale world, painted with dramatic depth of field.

Foreground: "the Executioner" charging toward the viewer in three-quarter view, mid-swing with a massive broad executioner's sword trailing ember sparks. Rusted iron plate armor, iron hood with two glowing ember eyes, chains and bone charms swinging, cloth shredded by heat.

Midground: a molten forge landscape: rivers of lava cracks in black stone, rusted iron towers and chimneys spewing fire, hanging chains, anvils. Voxel iron hounds with spiked backs leaping at the hero, a giant hammer-wielding blacksmith demon with a glowing furnace in its chest rising behind them, half hidden in smoke.

Sky: red-orange smoke clouds lit from below, falling ash, a dim dead sun.

Palette: ember orange #E07A2A, bright ember #F5B04A, rust #8A4A2B, iron #5A5F66 and #2E3136, wine blood #5E0E14, warm dark #241614, touches of bone #D9C9A3. No pure white or pure black.

Title: "GRIM CUBES" across the top, in a brutal forged-iron logo made of beveled cubes, glowing hot orange at the edges like metal fresh from the forge. No other text, no menus, no buttons, no UI.

Keep the right third of the image less busy (smoke and darker stone) so menu buttons can be placed there. Intense, loud, heavy-metal energy, grimdark.
```

## Prompt 3: O cavaleiro sombrio do fim dos tempos

Mais próximo da era medieval sombria das capas recentes: tom mais frio e solene, herói de costas diante de um exército e de uma fortaleza.

```text
Original dark-fantasy game cover painting, landscape 16:9. Mood inspired by medieval-hell heavy-metal shooter art: a lone armored knight facing an apocalyptic army under a burning sky, monumental scale, oppressive atmosphere. Original design only, no existing logos, characters or weapons.

The whole world is built from voxels: chunky cube characters, cube cliffs, cube fortresses, rendered as a detailed oil-painting-like illustration with atmospheric perspective.

Foreground, seen from behind and slightly from the side: "the Executioner" standing at the edge of a cliff of bone and stone, wide shoulders in rusted iron plates, heavy iron hood, a tattered dark cape moving in the wind, a huge executioner's sword planted point-down in the ground beside him, his hand on the hilt. A faint ember glow comes from under the hood.

Below him, a vast valley: an endless army of voxel undead and biomechanical flesh creatures marching between swamp trees and bone spires, banners of torn flesh, a nest of pulsing eggs, a crowned insect-like queen and a horned swamp matriarch towering in the distance among them.

Background: a ruined gothic fortress made of black iron and bone on the horizon, a giant eclipsed sun ringed with ember light, violet storm clouds, lightning.

Palette: night #14121C, violet shadows #2B2340, fog #3F3A52, iron #5A5F66, rust #8A4A2B, bone #D9C9A3, swamp green #2F4A33, flesh #6E2F3F, wine blood #5E0E14, with ember #E07A2A only on the sun ring, the eyes and a few torches. No pure white, no pure black.

Title: "GRIM CUBES" at the top center, carved into a heavy stone-and-iron logo built from cubes, cold grey with ember light in the cracks. No other text, no menus, no buttons, no UI.

Keep the lower-left area darker (the cliff shadow) so menu buttons can sit over it. Solemn, epic, grimdark, cinematic.
```
